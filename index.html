    Questions

    What facility managers ask us.

    What is an ATP test, and is it reliable?

    ATP (adenosine triphosphate) is found in all organic matter: food residue, body fluids, bacteria. A swab and a handheld meter give a reading in seconds, in Relative Light Units (RLU). Hospitals and food plants use it to verify cleaning. It doesn't identify specific germs. It shows whether a surface is truly clean, which is what you're paying for.

    Do I have to sign a long contract?

    No. Our agreement runs for 12 months, but either side can end it with 30 days' written notice and no penalty. Your first month includes a satisfaction guarantee.

    Who will actually clean my site?

    An UrbanShield employee, police-checked, trained and uniformed, plus a named backup who has already cleaned your site. We don't subcontract routine cleaning, and we don't use cash-paid labour.

    How do you handle keys and alarm codes?

    Keys carry a code tag only, never your name or address, and are signed in and out. Alarm codes are stored in an encrypted password vault, never written down. Arming is confirmed in our app every night.

    What if something isn't cleaned properly?

    Tell us by phone, text or email. We reply within 2 business hours and re-clean within 24 hours at no charge, then confirm with a photo or a re-test.

    Are your prices fixed?

    Yes, one monthly fee based on your site's hours, invoiced on the 1st. Prices adjust once a year on your anniversary (the greater of Ontario CPI or 3%), plus any government-mandated wage changes, always with written notice.

    Can you work around our hours?

    Yes. Night cleaning from 6 pm, early-morning retail crews from 5 am, weekend floor work, and day porters during business hours.
