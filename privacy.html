What we collect

When you use our booking or quote form, we collect what you enter: your name, business or site name, work email, phone number, type and size of space, area, your request, any message, and your price estimate. If you tick the optional box, we also record your consent to receive occasional emails.

Why we collect it

We do not sell or rent your information.

Form submissions are delivered through our form service (Formspree) to our business email, and kept in our customer records. These providers may store data outside Canada.
